Hot water, not cold — it sets the starch enough to stop the dough snapping back while you roll. The trim allowance covers scraps from cutting rounds; re-roll them once, then stop, because the third pass goes tough and those are the pierogi that split in the pot.

Freezer & pot

Freeze them apart on floured sheet pans until hard, two hours or so, then bag. Bagged soft, they weld into a brick. Boil from frozen in heavily salted water, in batches small enough that the water never stops moving; they are done a minute or two after they float.

Running order

kolejność
    Dough and filling figures follow standard Polish home and event-hall proportions and are per-piece, so they scale cleanly. Output rates cover assembly only — rolling, filling, sealing and racking — and assume the filling was made the day before and the dough is resting when the crew arrives. A drilled line beats these numbers; a kitchen doing it for the first time will not come close. Weigh the dough for the first dozen to calibrate, then work by eye.
